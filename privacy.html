冒險者道具袋 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 21 日

「冒險者道具袋」（以下稱「本 App」）是一款在裝置上運作的到期提醒工具。我們的原則很簡單：你的資料只在你的手機裡。

我們不蒐集的資料

本 App 不需要註冊或登入，也不會蒐集、上傳或分享任何個人資料，包括但不限於：姓名、電子郵件、電話、位置、裝置識別碼、聯絡人、照片、使用行為或分析數據。本 App 沒有廣告，也沒有第三方追蹤或分析工具。本 App 不會連上網路（App 內購買由 Apple／Google 處理，見下方）。

不需要證件號碼

本 App 只需要道具的名稱和日期，不需要、也不建議輸入護照、身分證、信用卡等證件的號碼。本 App 沒有證件號碼欄位；備註欄位也會提醒你不要填寫號碼。

存在你裝置上的資料

你輸入的道具名稱、到期日、週期、成員名稱、備註，以及提醒時間等設定，都只儲存在你的裝置上，不會傳送到我們或任何第三方的伺服器。刪除本 App 時，這些資料會一併刪除。

「備份碼」功能會把上述資料轉成一段文字，複製到你的剪貼簿。這段文字要存放或傳送到哪裡，完全由你決定；我們不會收到它。

通知

到期提醒由你的裝置在本機排程，不經過任何推播伺服器。你可以隨時在 App 內或系統設定中關閉通知。

App 內購買

「道具袋解鎖」的付款由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 處理，我們不會取得你的付款資訊。本 App 只會在裝置上記錄「已經解鎖」這一件事。相關資料的處理方式，請參考 Apple 與 Google 的隱私權政策。

不是醫療或法律建議

本 App 只提醒你輸入的日期。預填的週期（例如濾心、疫苗、汽機車定檢）只是常見建議，請依實際規定、產品說明或醫師指示修改。

兒童隱私

本 App 不蒐集任何人的個人資料，包括 13 歲以下的兒童。

政策變更

如果本政策有變更，我們會更新本頁面並修改上方的生效日期。若未來的版本需要蒐集任何資料，會先在 App 內清楚告知並取得你的同意。

Adventurer's Bag Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Adventurer's Bag ("the App") is an expiry reminder that runs on your device. Our principle is simple: your data stays on your phone.

Data we do not collect

The App needs no sign-up or login, and does not collect, upload or share any personal data, including but not limited to your name, email, phone number, location, device identifiers, contacts, photos, usage or analytics data. The App has no ads and no third-party tracking or analytics. The App does not connect to the internet (in-app purchases are handled by Apple / Google, see below).

No ID numbers needed

The App only needs an item's name and dates. You don't need to, and shouldn't, enter passport, ID or credit card numbers. The App has no field for document numbers, and the note field reminds you not to enter them.

Data stored on your device

The item names, expiry dates, intervals, member names, notes and settings such as reminder times are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or any third-party server. Deleting the App deletes this data.

The "backup code" feature turns this data into a piece of text copied to your clipboard. Where you keep or send that text is entirely up to you; we never receive it.

Notifications

Expiry reminders are scheduled locally on your device and do not go through any push server. You can turn them off at any time in the App or in your system settings.

In-app purchase

Payment for the full version is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never receive your payment details. The App only records on your device that the full version was purchased. Please refer to Apple's and Google's privacy policies for how they handle that data.

Not medical or legal advice

The App only reminds you of the dates you enter. Pre-filled intervals (such as filters, vaccines and vehicle inspections) are common suggestions; please adjust them to the actual rules, product manuals or your doctor's advice.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version ever needs to collect any data, we will tell you clearly in the App and ask for your consent first.
